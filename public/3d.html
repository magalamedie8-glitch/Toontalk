ToonTalk 3D prototype 2

Script commands
Cast: [Kato is a boy] · [Mama is a gomesi woman] · [Rex is a dog] · [Fido is a real fox] · [Ali is a my model]
Moves: [Name walks|runs to left|right|center|door|near|far|Othername] · [Name turns to Other] · [Name waves|jumps|dances|nods]
World: [scene: room|street|classroom|village] · [add car at left] · [car drives to right] · [remove car] · [camera: auto|wide|close|two] · [pause: 1]
Voices: pick a voice on each character card. Lips follow the real voice audio.
Free models used: Soldier, Michelle (Mixamo), RobotExpressive (CC0), Fox (CC-BY 4.0, PixelMannen / @tomkranis / Asobo), Horse (three.js examples). Check licences before selling videos that show them.
